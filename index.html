About the Book

Is the fashion industry at a breaking point? The solution lies in reimagining how we create, own, and connect.

As the world's second-largest polluter, the fashion industry's "take-make-dispose" model is failing. The Future Thread reveals how the convergence of Web3 and Artificial Intelligence transforms fashion from a linear supply chain into an intelligent, decentralized ecosystem.

Who this book is for: Whether you are a legacy brand executive, an independent designer, or a technologist, The Future Thread provides the blueprint to build a smarter, sustainable, and decentralized future.

The fashion revolution starts now. Fashion is no longer dictated by a centralized few. Tomorrow's fashion houses are decentralized communities, funded by fans and managed on-chain. From optimizing supply chains to launching virtual runways, The Future Thread equips you to lead in an era where finance, identity, and style converge.

The fabric of the industry is being rewoven. Are you dressed for the future?

Gawain "G2" Bracy II

Author · Founder & CEO, Creative Platform, Inc.

Gawain "G2" Bracy II is a visionary technologist, music producer, and Web3 strategist with over a decade of experience building scalable applications and empowering creators. As the Founder and CEO of Creative Platform, Inc., he leads the development of decentralized solutions designed to shift power back to the creator.

His career is rooted in a deep understanding of the entertainment industry. Starting as a signed music producer for Disney and Interscope Records, he later founded G2 Music Group, where he developed international talent and navigated the complexities of corporate communications and music partnerships. This firsthand experience highlighted the gaps in traditional models, driving him to pioneer digital platforms that prioritize true ownership, transparency, and practical, creative-centric solutions.

A dedicated blockchain innovator, Gawain has spearheaded smart contract architecture, decentralized autonomous organizations (DAOs), and global ecosystem partnerships. He holds degrees in Mobile Development, New Media Communications, and Music Business from Full Sail University.

Operating out of Jacksonville, Florida, Gawain continues to bridge the gap between creative storytelling and cutting-edge technology, always striving to keep his work aligned with his core values of balance, empathy, and simplicity.

Co-authored with Diana Cañas and Mery Wayfarer.
